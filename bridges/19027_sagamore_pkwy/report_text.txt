6/3/2021: This bridge is over in good conditions. The damages that have happened previously were structurally
addressed. However, another paint job is needed to take care of scratches that probably happened after repair
work. No work is planned in SPMS for the structure and no Maintenance letter was made. (Al Naderi)
Previous Inspection
Bridge was struck on 1/4/2018 that resulted in a critical finding. Correspondence attached. National Bridge
Inventory form is included for information only; no values were changed by this report. (NP 1/4/2018)
  On the afternoon of 8/31/2015, I was notified by Customer Service from Crawfordsville District that this
bridge had been hit by a Lafayette sanitation truck. The officer did not believe the bridge was in danger, so I
checked the bridge on my way home. This bridge has been hit many times, especially by SB traffic. The
sanitation truck hit the north facia beam over the SB lane of North 9th Street. The bottom flange is deflected
approximately 12 inches toward the south. I was not able to observe any cracks. I am certain this is the
location of the impact because of the fresh (not yet rusted) scrapes as well as the presence of hydraulic fluid on
many of the beams just south of this location. I asked that Central Office determine how much deflection is
acceptable. That correspondence is attached to this report. The National Bridge Inventory form is included for
information only; no values were changed by this report. (NP 8/31/2015)
